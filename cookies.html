Cookie Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 July 2026

We use browser storage and similar technologies. Optional services remain blocked until you consent. You can reject optional services without losing access to the main website.

Necessary storage

tnua-consent is first-party local storage containing your choices, the policy version and the date selected. It remains until you clear browser storage or replace your selection. It does not track activity across websites.

Analytics

If you choose Analytics, Google Analytics may set cookies including _ga and related measurement identifiers, generally for up to two years, to distinguish visits and generate aggregated usage reports. Google may receive your IP address, device/browser information, pages viewed and interaction data. Advertising storage and personalisation are disabled by this site.

External media

If you choose External media, embedded YouTube and Vimeo players may receive your IP address, device/browser information and page context and may set their own cookies or local storage. Until permission is given, the players are replaced by a privacy placeholder and no request is made to those providers.

Changing your choice

Use “Cookie settings” below at any time. Withdrawing consent prevents further optional loading and removes analytics cookies accessible to this website. Your browser or a provider may retain information already transmitted or storage that this website cannot directly remove.
