Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 24 July 2026

T-NUA Studio is working to make this website usable by as many people as possible. Our target is Israeli Standard 5568, based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA.

Accessibility features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, controls and visible focus indicators.
  • Semantic page regions, headings, labels and a skip-to-content link.
  • Support for browser zoom, narrow screens, reduced-motion preferences and screen readers.
  • Optional third-party media remains blocked until selected and includes a direct provider link.

Known limitations

Some portfolio videos may not yet include complete captions, audio descriptions or text transcripts. Some third-party YouTube and Vimeo interfaces are controlled by their providers. If you need an accessible text description or alternative version of any portfolio item, contact us and we will provide a reasonable alternative.

This statement does not claim that every page has completed an independent manual conformance audit. We continue to test and improve the site.

Feedback and accessible alternatives

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, tell us which page or content caused difficulty, the assistive technology or browser used if relevant, and the format you need. Email inf@t-nua.studio. We aim to acknowledge accessibility requests within five business days.
