Yad Mordechai Olive Oil

CGI social media campaign for a premium Israeli olive oil brand

Category
CGI Animation
Format
Social Media Video
Services
3D, Animation, VFX
Client
Yad Mordechai

The Challenge

Yad Mordechai needed a visually compelling social media campaign to communicate the premium quality and heritage of their olive oil. The goal was to translate the richness of the product into a short-form CGI video that would stop the scroll and feel as elevated as the brand itself.

The Creative Approach

We centered the animation on the product itself—using photorealistic 3D rendering, fluid dynamics, and carefully composed lighting to bring out the deep color, texture, and natural character of the olive oil. Every detail was crafted to feel authentic and appetizing.

The Production

Our team built a high-detail 3D model of the bottle and packaging, developed custom fluid simulations for the oil pour sequence, and designed lighting setups that emphasize warmth and organic quality. The animation was optimized for vertical and square social formats.

The Result

A clean, elegant campaign video that positions Yad Mordechai Olive Oil as a premium product—ready for Instagram, Facebook, and digital advertising placements. The CGI approach allowed precise control over every visual element while keeping production flexible and efficient.

Deliverables

  • Photorealistic 3D bottle and packaging model
  • Fluid simulation and oil pour animation
  • Color grading and post-production
  • Vertical format (9:16) for Stories / Reels
  • Square format (1:1) for feed posts
  • Multiple resolution exports for digital use
