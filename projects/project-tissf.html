TISSF 2024

Festival installation bringing cultural celebration to life with dynamic CGI animation

Category
Festival Installation
Format
CGI Animation Installation
Services
3D Animation, Projection, Design
Event
TISSF 2024 Festival

The Challenge

Create an eye-catching festival installation that celebrates cultural traditions through contemporary digital art. The challenge was to blend festive energy with sophisticated animation that would engage diverse audiences throughout the event.

The Creative Direction

We developed a vibrant, dynamic CGI animation that incorporates traditional cultural elements with modern visual language. The animation loops were designed to maintain visual interest throughout the festival while remaining visually cohesive with the event's aesthetic.

The Production

Our team created multiple animation sequences, optimized rendering for large-scale projection, coordinated installation logistics, and ensured technical reliability for the duration of the event. Each element was crafted to withstand festival conditions while maintaining visual excellence.

The Celebration

The TISSF 2024 installation became a focal point of the festival, attracting large crowds and generating significant social media coverage. Attendees frequently photographed and shared content, extending the installation's reach far beyond the physical event.

Deliverables

  • Multiple animation sequences (custom loops)
  • 4K animation rendering optimized for projection
  • Projection mapping and display system setup
  • Technical support and on-site management
  • Backup systems for reliability
  • Festival signage and wayfinding integration
