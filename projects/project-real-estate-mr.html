Real Estate MR Experience

AI-powered Mixed Reality Personalised Experience for property walkthroughs

Category
Immersive MR
Format
Interactive App
Services
UI/UX, 3D Web, WebAR, AI Layouts
Duration
Interactive

The Challenge

Real estate sales are shifting faster than many professionals realize. Potential buyers struggle to visualize properties long before they are built, and traditional renders or 2D floor plans fail to convey the true scale, lighting, and feel of a space, leading to longer sales cycles and lower conversion rates.

The Creative Approach

To solve this, we conceptualized an AI-powered Mixed Reality Personalised Experience. Instead of static mockups or passive video walkthroughs, we put the future directly into the hands of the buyers, allowing them to use their own phone to see and walk through any property at true scale.

The Execution

The application enables users to explore how a project integrates with its surroundings at different hours of the day, understand the full layout, and move through each room. This provides a clear understanding of how the space will actually function, whether residential, office, or commercial.

The Result

The impact of this technology is clear and immediate: stronger lead qualification, higher conversion rates, and a significantly shorter sales cycle. For real estate teams, it is not just an upgrade—it is a competitive edge.

Deliverables

  • WebAR & Mobile Mixed Reality application integration
  • Interactive scale modeling & spatial placement
  • Real-time time-of-day illumination simulation
  • Immersive interior walkthroughs at true scale
  • AI-assisted layout personalization interface
  • Performance-optimized 3D asset optimization
